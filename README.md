# 👨🏼‍💻 About Me

🎓 **Physics Undergraduate at the Federal University of Alfenas — UNIFAL-MG**

🔬 I am interested in the intersection between **physics, machine learning, materials science, and computational modeling**, combining theoretical reasoning with experimental practice to investigate complex physical systems.

🧪 Currently, I work with **machine learning applied to materials science**, especially using **XGBoost and multivariate regression models** to predict and analyze material properties. My research interests include the use of data-driven methods to support the discovery, characterization, and optimization of advanced materials.

⚛️ I am a member of the **Laboratory for the Development of Theoretical Physics — LDFT/UNIFAL**, where I develop interests related to theoretical physics, computational tools, and scientific modeling. I also participate in the **Advanced Materials Group — UNIFAL**, working on both theoretical and experimental aspects of materials research.

🧫 On the experimental side, I have experience with the **synthesis of perovskite materials**, exploring how preparation methods, structure, and physical properties are connected. I am especially interested in how experimental data and machine learning models can complement each other in materials research.

💻 My background also includes **embedded systems, microcontrollers, automation, instrumentation, and web application development**. Although these areas are currently more connected to personal projects and hobbies, they continue to shape the way I approach problems: building practical tools, automating processes, and connecting physical systems with computational solutions.

🚀 My main goal is to contribute to research and development in **machine learning for materials science, experimental physics, and computational modeling**, using programming, data analysis, and physical intuition to solve scientific and technological challenges.
